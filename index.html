Change the figures

What can be dragged is shaded when a slide opens and while the pointer is over the figure. Over a shaded line the pointer becomes ↔ or ↕ and names the quantity.

On a touch screen, drag a shaded line with a finger and tap ‹ › to move.

Where the pointer becomes a hand, click: words, dots, the letters P and Q.

The line under each figure lists what you can change there and the keys that do the same. Hold Shift for larger steps.
