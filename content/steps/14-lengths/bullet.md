**Q:** How does the pinning interval depend on the lengths, and what are its limits?

- $a_\pm$ explicit; surfaces over $\tanh L_1$, $\tanh(L_2/2)$; zero length → $\tfrac12$
- both long: $[\tfrac13,\tfrac23]$; $\tfrac13$ = same criterion (one into two), $\tfrac23$ = reverse orientation (JM)
- only pairs long, $a\ne\tfrac12$ in $(\tfrac13,\tfrac23)$: positive minimum single-edge length (McKean counterpart of IJM)
