**Q:** Why can we compute exactly, without assuming small heterogeneity?

- McKean: $-u$ below $a$, $1-u$ above; balanced at $\tfrac12$
- linear on each side → manifolds straight up to $u=a$ (formula)
- drag $u=a$: lines fixed, ends slide along $u=a$
- ends meet → a front; enough to decide which $a$ give monotone fronts
