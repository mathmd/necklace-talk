### Why one front is stable and the other is not

::: build 0-1
$u^P$: interface moved to $\rho_-<\rho_P<\rho_+$
:::

::: build 1-1
$$B_\rho=V_\rho-\bigl(b(\rho)-a\bigr)$$
$$(pB_\rho')'+f(B_\rho)=b(\rho)-a$$
$b(\rho_-)>a$: subsolution below $u^P$
$b(\rho_+)<a$: supersolution above $u^P$
:::

::: build 2
$u^Q$: joined at $\rho_Q$, at $a\pm\sigma$
$$(p\underline u_\sigma')'+f(\underline u_\sigma)=J_+\,\delta_{\rho_Q}$$
$$(p\overline u_\sigma')'+f(\overline u_\sigma)=J_-\,\delta_{\rho_Q}$$
$J_\pm=(h^s-h^u)(a\pm\sigma)$, $\;J_+>0>J_-$

both constructions read the orientation encoded by $b'$
:::
