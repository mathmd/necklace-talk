**Q:** Trajectories cross each other; how do we get a picture we can reason with?

- trajectories cross → sample at $x=s+kL$
- $\Psi^s$: one sample to the next
- two computed monotone fronts, green and purple, give two displayed
  orbits of samples; no exhaustive cubic count is claimed
- drag a red line ($s$): fronts fixed, samples move; phase in the corner
- varying $a$ numerically continues the displayed fronts while they are found
