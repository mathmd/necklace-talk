### Line: the front moves. Junction: it can stop.

$f(u)=u(1-u)(u-a)$

one edge into $n\ge2$ identical edges,
$F(u)=\int_0^u f$, $F(1)>0$:
a front stops iff
$$F(1)+(n^2-1)F(a)\le0$$

$n=2$: $\ 0.373\lesssim a<\tfrac12$

*Jimbo–Morita 2019, 2021*
