**Q:** On a homogeneous line, which state wins? Can a junction alone stop the same front?

- numerical simulations on the line and junction, with front value
  shown as height; cubic $f(u)=u(1-u)(u-a)$
- line: sign of $\int_0^1 f$; $a<\tfrac12$ → $1$ invades; moves on
- junction: same front stops at the vertex
- move $a$: line always moves; junction stops for $a\ge a_*$, $a_*\approx0.37$ ($0.3731803$; blocking includes equality) ($0<a<\tfrac12$)
- Jimbo–Morita: $F=\int_0^u f$, $F(1)>0$, $n\ge2$: $F(1)+(n^2-1)F(a)\le0$
