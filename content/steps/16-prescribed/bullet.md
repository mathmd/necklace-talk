**Q:** Can we write a monotone front down?

- The bounded linear solution for the prescribed source
  $\mathbf1_{\{x<\rho\}}$ is $V_\rho$.
  We then check whether its threshold set agrees with the prescribed source.
- $V_\rho(\rho)=b(\rho)$; front $\iff b(\rho)=a$
- drag the red line ($\rho$): hits $a$ at $\rho_P$, $\rho_Q$; continues into the next period
- drag the dashed line ($a$): roots move (two inside; one per period at an endpoint); $V_\rho$ itself does not depend on $a$
