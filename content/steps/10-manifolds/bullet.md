**Q:** Which orbits go from $(1,0)$ to $(0,0)$?

- A common point of $W^u(1,0)$ and $W^s(0,0)$ samples a stationary front.
  Samples on the same common orbit represent the same front
  modulo period translations.
- The plotted cubic manifolds and their intersections are computed.
- On the homogeneous line at $a=1/2$, the relevant separatrices coincide.
  The displayed periodic cubic computation finds intersections
  over a range of thresholds.
- This numerical continuation gives neither a proved classification
  of the cubic fronts nor their full existence interval.
