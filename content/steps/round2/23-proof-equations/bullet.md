- Fix an interior threshold and an odd number $m\ge3$ of crossings
  in prescribed periods $N_1<\cdots<N_m$.
  Write $\rho_i=N_iL+s_i$, $0\le s_i<L$,
  and $n=\min_i(N_{i+1}-N_i)$.
- Set $\theta_i=a$ for odd $i$ and $\theta_i=1-a$ for even $i$.
  The number $\lambda>1$ is the unstable Floquet multiplier per period;
  the tail decay rate is $\log\lambda/L$.
- The alternating sum is a front exactly when all matching equations hold.
  For $n\ge2$, the remainders satisfy $|R_i|\le C_m\lambda^{-n}$.
- Near each product of simple roots of $b(s_i)=\theta_i$,
  the remainders and their first phase derivatives are small.
  Contraction gives one nearby solution per word for sufficiently large $n$.
  Exclusion away from those roots gives the exact $2^m$ count.
